## Audit

Every venue the 2026-11-17 run called 'wrong shape' or 'off', checked by eye on `cache/overview_small.jpg` after candidate selection was fixed (see `Docs/Track-Reference.md` - Reading the verdict). 'Wrong' says which side is at fault: the game's track, or the reference candidate the 2026-11-17 run measured it against. Candidate numbers are the ones in each venue's current `report.md`; `--write-trace=<#>` takes them. No track has been changed. In all eleven the game's line is wrong; in five the old reference was wrong too (a chain through a pit lane or another circuit's roads), and two committed traces (Atlanta, Kansas) are such chains and must be replaced, not imported. 2026-11-22: every venue measured in Parts 1 and 2 (the row-244 split) that was not already 'matches' is rebuilt and now matches - Road America, Chicago, Indianapolis, Sonoma, Mexico City, Portland, Talladega, IRP and Rockingham (0.4-2.7 m RMS). Left from that set: San Diego only (unmeasured - OSM has no closed ring for the Coronado street course; it needs a reference_ways.json like Chicago's, and its real lap is 3.4 mi against the game's 3.0). Left elsewhere: COTA, Charlotte and Darlington (traces written, Unity import pending), CharlotteRoval (rebuild from #0 after checking the 2024 infield), and eight venues not measured yet (Miami, Michigan, MidOhio, Milwaukee, Nashville, NewHampshire, NorthWilkesboro, Phoenix).
