# Text size audit

Generated by `Draftmaster > UI > Audit Text Sizes` (`Assets/Editor/TextSizeAudit.cs`). Lists every uGUI
`Text` / `TextMeshProUGUI` that comes out under the type scale's floor — **24px on a 1920x1080 screen**
(Label, 8 kit px; see `Docs/Type-Scale.md`). World-space text and IMGUI panels are not measured.
Inactive rows are often templates or legacy UI; they still count once something turns them on.

181 texts measured in 85 prefabs and the open scene(s) `Assets/Menus/TeamGarage.unity`; **100 under the floor** in 10 assets.
